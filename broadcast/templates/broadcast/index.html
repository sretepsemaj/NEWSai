Welcome to NEWSai, an LLM-driven news processing system!

Where LLMs are used to sift through vast amounts of data to make all types of news outlets possible. Imagine taking twelve different angles on one event, sourcing your information, and delivering both sides of the story, including each perspective’s concerns and grievances.

This is just a demo, to prove that LLMs can be the writers, curators, and cataloguers for comprehensive news coverage. Responsible use is key to accurate, objective criticism.

Workflow: The Reporter creates the story, the Director makes the teleprompter script, and the Anchorman delivers the news to the audience—even personalizing it if desired.

Have fun exploring these functionalities!

  • Reporter will find the political story
  • Director will create the teleprompter script
  • Anchorman will deliver the story
  • Paperboy will take the news to the people
